Part 2. Checks on every screen you visit:
- Text size: is any text smaller than 14px (or 12px for tiny labels)? Is body text comfortable to read? Is anything cut off or squeezed at 390px wide, or at 200% browser zoom?
- Contrast: any text or icons hard to see against the dark background? Estimate the contrast ratio for grey text (it should be at least 4.5:1).
- Buttons and tap targets: is anything you can tap smaller than 44 × 44px? Are things too close together to tap the right one? Are the main buttons within easy thumb reach at the bottom of the screen, or far away at the top?
- Is it clear what can be tapped and what can't (buttons vs plain text)?
- Forms: does every field have a visible label (not just a placeholder)? Are errors shown next to the field, in words? Does the keyboard type suit the field (number pad for numbers)? Can you move between fields with Tab and submit with Enter?
- Keyboard and screen reader: can you reach and use every control with the keyboard alone (Tab, Enter, Space)? Is the focus visible? Do buttons, switches and choices have names, and do they say on/off or selected? Do headings exist on each page?
- Colour alone: is anything only shown by colour (a dot, a red word) without words or an icon?
- Motion and timing: anything that moves or disappears too fast to read (messages that vanish)?
- Consistency: do the tops of pages follow one pattern (main tabs: big title, icons on the right; inner pages: back arrow then title; rooms: ⌄ · name · ⋯ · Report)?

Report format:
- Hard to find or do (task, taps, what went wrong, a suggested fix), most serious first.
- Accessibility problems (page, the exact element, what's wrong, which guideline: WCAG 2.2 where you can).
- Easy to find and use (what works well).
Keep it plain and specific: name the page and the exact wording or button.
