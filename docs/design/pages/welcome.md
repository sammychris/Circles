# Welcome

**Kind:** screen · **Release:** V1 · **Entry:** first launch, and after logging out

## Purpose
In one screen, tell a new person what Circles is and what it isn't, then get them to sign up.

## Layout (Night theme, full screen)
1. App name **Circles** small at the top (`heading`, `ember`).
2. Illustration made of shapes: a small ring of 5 avatar circles around a soft ember glow (the "lamp"). One avatar has the live-green speaking ring.
3. Headline (`display`): "Come in and feel better with people"
4. Three short rows with icons (`textSoft` body):
   - users: "Small voice rooms. Everyone gets to talk."
   - eye-off: "No likes, no followers, no public numbers."
   - shield: "Nickname in front. Hosts and rules keep it kind."
5. Primary: **Get started** → Phone number.
6. Tertiary: **I already have an account** → Phone number (same flow; the app detects existing accounts).
7. Small print (`meta`): "By continuing you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy." with both as links. "18+ only."

## Rules
- No carousel; one screen. No video.
- If opened from an invite link, add a line above the headline: "Ada_K invited you to Night Owls." and after sign-up go straight to Circle detail.
