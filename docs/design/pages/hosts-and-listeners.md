# Hosts, listeners and teachers (the next phase)

**Kind:** plan for a whole phase · **Decided with Sammy:** 9 October 2026 · **Build order:** `docs/CIRCLES_BUILD_PLAN.md` › Phase 2
Read with `CIRCLES_INTENT.md` › Helping, teaching and earning, `paid-groups.md` (payments), `room-host-view.md` (host tools in a room) and `moderation-console.md` (Sammy's approvals).

## Why

Besides games, two things matter most in Circles:
1. **People who are down** and need someone to talk to.
2. **People who want to learn** something new while they talk.

Both need good people on the other side: hosts who keep support rooms kind, listeners who give one-on-one time, and teachers who explain things. This phase builds the path for those people. They help for free first, earn trust, and can later earn money on Circles. That is also a reason for people to come to Circles: **"Help people, grow your name, and earn when you're ready."**

**Help is never locked behind money.** Group support rooms and the crisis help line are always free, for everyone.

## The ladder

| Step | Who | What they can do | How they get there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Member** | Anyone (18+, nickname) | Join any room, including support rooms. | Sign up. |
| **Host** | Anyone willing to help and a good listener | Host free support groups, and schedule them. Host tools in the room (lower hands, mute, remove). | Apply, read the host guide, pass the host quiz, and Sammy approves. |
| **Listener** | A host who has proven themselves | Everything a host does, plus **one-on-one sessions**: a "Talk one-on-one" button on their card, their own price, their own times. | Earned (see "From host to listener"), then Sammy approves. |
| **Teacher** | Someone who teaches a language or skill | Free practice rooms (anyone can already), plus **paid classes and sessions** with the Table as their board. | The same idea as listeners: teach free first, good reviews, then Sammy approves. |

**Names on screen:** "Host", "Listener", "Teacher". Never "professional", "therapist" or "counsellor" unless Circles has checked that person is truly licensed. That is a separate badge, decided later, with a legal check first.

## Becoming a host

1. **Me › Help others › Become a host.** A short page: what a host does, what a host never does, "Hosting is free and unpaid. Hosts who help well can later offer one-on-one sessions and earn."
2. **The basics:** the account is at least 14 days old, has joined at least 3 rooms, and has no upheld reports. (Numbers are defaults, open to change.)
3. **The host guide:** short screens on how to listen without fixing, how to keep a room kind, when and how to share the crisis help, what to do if someone is in danger, and the rules (no selling, no contact details, no advice on medicine).
4. **The host quiz:** about 10 questions, mostly "what would you do?" situations, for example:
   - "Someone says they don't want to live any more. What do you do first?"
   - "One person keeps talking over someone who is crying."
   - "Someone in the room asks for your phone number."
   - "Someone asks what medicine they should take."
   It's multiple choice, with the right answer explained afterwards. The safety questions must all be right; the rest need about 8 out of 10. It can be retaken after 7 days.
   - **Not an IQ test.** Cleverness doesn't show whether someone listens kindly or stays calm. Situation questions test what matters here. (Sammy asked for a test of how equipped people are; this is that test.)
5. **Three short written answers:** why they want to host, a time they helped someone, and how they look after themselves after a hard conversation.
6. **Sammy approves** in the console's host applications (`moderation-console.md` › 3), or declines with a kind reason. Approved hosts get the "Host" chip.

## Hosting free support groups

- **No host, no support room.** In a room where everyone is down and nobody guides it, one person's despair can pull others lower, or someone unkind can hurt people. The host keeps it kind. (This was already the rule; it stays.)
- Rooms need at least 3 people, counting the host (unchanged).
- **Hosts can schedule support groups** ("Feeling low? Tuesday 8 pm"), once or every week, using the scheduled rooms already built. Only hosts can schedule support rooms; for everyone else they stay unschedulable. Scheduled support groups show in the support door under "Or pick a time", **never in Explore**. Reminders for them never name the room on the lock screen; they only say "Your Circles room starts in 15 minutes".
- **Nobody may sell in a support room.** No "book me", no prices, no links to outside services. It can be reported ("Selling or asking for money") and costs a host their status.

## From host to listener

A host can ask to become a listener once they have, by default:
- hosted at least **10 support sessions** over at least **4 weeks**,
- received at least **20 "this helped me" thank-yous** (the existing thank-you, counted privately),
- **no upheld reports** in the last 60 days,
- and, once reviews exist, an average of at least 4.5.

Then **Sammy approves**. Before a listener can be **paid** (later), Circles also checks their identity and payout account; payment providers need this anyway.

The host sees their own progress privately in Me › Help others ("7 of 10 sessions hosted"). Nobody else sees it. This is not a ranking.

## One-on-one sessions

**How someone finds a listener:** in a support group, each listener's card (tap their face) shows **"Talk one-on-one"**, their price and their free part. Everyone in the room can see the button on the listener's card, but **who taps it stays private**: nobody in the room ever knows, and the listener only learns about it if the person actually sends a request. Later, the support door can also list available listeners.

**The person asks, the listener never sells.** A listener may not mention their sessions or prices in a support group. The button on their card is the only way.

**Booking:**
1. The person taps **Talk one-on-one** and sees the listener's nickname, what they help with, the free part, the price after it, and the session length.
2. They pick **"Now"** (if the listener is free) or one of the listener's open times.
3. The listener accepts (or the time is already open), and both get a reminder.

**The session:**
- A private voice room for two, in the support door. It follows every support-room rule: nobody sees you're there, no games, no photos, mic off by default, crisis help always one tap away.
- **A short free start** (default 15 minutes; a listener may offer more free time, or make whole sessions free). The person sees a gentle timer.
- **Until payments exist, sessions are free and end at the free time.** A short note says "You can book another time".
- **When payments exist:** before the free part ends, the person sees "Continue for [PRICE]?" with **Continue** and **End here**. Nothing is charged unless they tap Continue. A free session can never become paid without that choice, and nobody is asked for money in the middle of talking.
- Either side can end at any time. Block and Report are always there. Nothing is recorded.
- **Contact details are blocked**: phone numbers, emails, links and @names in chat are hidden, and the Report reasons include **"Asked me to pay outside Circles"**. Anyone who takes clients off Circles loses listener status.

**Why not "one room with different settings"?** If anyone could start a one-on-one support room, a harmful person could set one up and wait for someone vulnerable. So one-on-one only exists when one side is an approved listener. Ordinary members can never offer it.

## Teachers and the Table as a teaching board

- **Free practice rooms** stay open to everyone (already built).
- **Teachers** earn their status the same way: teach free sessions, collect good reviews, then Sammy approves.
- **Paid classes and sessions:** monthly classes as in `paid-groups.md`, plus single paid sessions ("Igbo for beginners, Saturday 10 am, [PRICE]"). Learners pay to join.
- **The Table becomes the teacher's board:**
  - **Slides:** the teacher puts several pictures up and moves through them with Next and Back.
  - **A pointer:** they can point at part of the picture, and everyone sees where.
  - **Writing on it:** a simple pen for underlining and quick drawings (shared with Draw and Guess later).
  - **Only the teacher puts things up** in a class, unless they hand the Table to a learner.
  - Screen sharing and notes already work and stay.

## Reviews

Sammy's decision (9 October 2026), an exception to "no scores or rankings":
- Reviews exist **only for hosts, listeners and teachers**, never for ordinary members.
- After a one-on-one session, a paid class or session, a person can give 1 to 5 stars and an optional short note.
- **On screen, only kind summaries:** "Helped 40 people", "4.8 from 25 sessions". There are no leaderboards and no "top listeners" lists, and nobody sees who wrote which review.
- Sammy (and the approval rules) use the details privately.
- Notes are checked before anyone else sees them (later).

## Money (later, when Sammy decides)

- Payments come with Paystack or similar. That needs a business account, payouts and refunds (`paid-groups.md`).
- The listener or teacher sets their price. Circles keeps a share (the percentage is Sammy's decision) and pays the rest out.
- **Before charging for support:** check what Nigerian rules say about paid emotional support and counselling, and about holding money for others. Don't guess on money or safety.
- **Always free:** group support rooms, the crisis help line, free practice rooms, and every room where people just talk or play.

## Safety, all in one place

1. No host, no support room.
2. One-on-one only with an approved listener.
3. Who asks for one-on-one is private.
4. No selling in support rooms; the person asks, the listener never pitches.
5. Price and free part shown before booking; never asked for money while talking; nothing charged without a tap.
6. Contact details blocked; "Asked me to pay outside Circles" is a report reason.
7. Nobody sees anyone is in a support room or a one-on-one, not even friends.
8. The crisis help line is always one tap away and always free.
9. Sammy approves every host, listener and teacher, and can remove the status at any time.
10. Only nicknames, everywhere.
