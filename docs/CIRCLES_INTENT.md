# Circles — Product Intent

This file explains **what Circles is for and how it should feel**. It guides layout and flow.
The visual style comes from the existing UI and design skills; keep that as it is.

---

## What Circles is

**A place where you come in and feel better with people.**
Small voice rooms of real people: talk, play, make friends, get support, learn together.
Voice comes first. Video is an option, never required.

## How it should feel

Like walking into **one warm house where everything happens**.
- Nobody feels out of place, whether they're bored, curious, lonely, or down.
- Nothing feels hidden. Every option is easy to see from the home screen.
- It feels kind and calm, not loud and competitive.

## Where it started (most important)

Circles began with **people who need someone to talk to**.
Everything else (games, topics, learning) brings people in, but that need is the heart of the app.

---

## Home screen: one house, many doors

Each door leads to the same thing: a small room of real people with their voices on.

| Door | What happens |
|---|---|
| **I'm bored** | Join a room and play a game together while talking |
| **I want to talk** | Chat rooms on topics and moods |
| **I need someone to talk to** | A calm, kind room for people who are down |
| **Learn together** | Language and topic groups |
| **My people** | Rooms with friends you've made |

### The "I need someone to talk to" door
- It is **always visible on the home screen** and never buried under games.
- It stands out **gently**: a soft, warm line or link, e.g. *"Need someone to talk to? Come in →"*, not a loud alarm-style button.
- Its rooms are quieter, with gentler rules and kind hosts, and no game-room noise.
- Inside, there is a quiet, easy way to reach professional help for anyone in crisis.

## Games

**Purpose:** games give people an excuse to gather. The conversation is the real product.

Use existing, proven game code plugged into rooms where possible, instead of building games from scratch.

### Now (launch)
- **Ludo**
- **Draughts**
- **Chess** (in teams)
- **Whot**
- **Find the Impostor**: everyone gets the same secret word except one person; describe it, then vote on who's faking. Naija words (suya, danfo, NEPA).
- **Mafia / Werewolf**: secret "bad guys", group argues and votes each round.

### Next
- **Draw and Guess**: one person draws on the Table, everyone guesses (needs the Table first).
- **Ayo**: the traditional seed game.
- **Finish the Line**: complete the Nollywood quote, pidgin proverb, or famous line.
- **Story Chain**: one sentence each, with a twist card mid-round.
- **On the Same Wave**: one clue, the team debates where it lands on a scale.

### Later
- Games from partner game makers, once Circles has active rooms, so their game runs inside a room while people play, watch, and talk.
- Heavier games (football, fighting games, anything graphics-heavy).

## Helping, teaching and earning (Sammy, 9 October 2026)

Besides games, two things matter most: **people who are down** and **people who want to learn**. Both need good people on the other side, and Circles gives them a path. Full plan: `docs/design/pages/hosts-and-listeners.md`.

- **The ladder:** member → **host** (hosts free support groups) → **listener** (one-on-one sessions) or **teacher** (paid classes and sessions).
  - Anyone willing to help can apply to host. They read a short guide, pass a "what would you do?" quiz, and Sammy approves.
  - Hosts who help well over time (many sessions, many "this helped me" thank-yous, no reports) can become listeners, again approved by Sammy. Teachers earn their status the same way.
- **Support door, two ways in:**
  - **Free support groups:** always free, always with a host, at least 3 people. Hosts can schedule them.
  - **One-on-one with a listener:** a short free start, then paid through Circles (once payments exist). The person asks privately; listeners never sell in groups.
- **Free groups are where trust is built.** Listeners meet people by helping for free, and people who want more can ask for one-on-one. This brings helpers to Circles too: "Help people, grow your name, and earn when you're ready."
- **Learning:** teachers use the Table as their board (slides, pointer, pen) and can run paid classes or single sessions.
- **Reviews** exist only for hosts, listeners and teachers, shown as kind summaries, never as leaderboards.
- **Help is never locked behind money.** Group support rooms and the crisis help line are always free.

## Already decided (details in the Circles plan doc)

- Free peer rooms: 6–7 people, everyone talks, nobody broadcasts.
- Paid hosted rooms: host sets the fee; default 10, max 12.
- Phone-verified, but shown only by a chosen nickname.
- No adverts in the talking part.
- **The Table**: a shared screen everyone in a room sees.
- Circles Plus: small monthly plan for extra features (details later).
- Payments and gifts come later.

## Still open

- **Link-first access:** the first visit opens from a link in the browser with no download; the app comes later for people who stay.
- How each door's rooms are matched and filled.
